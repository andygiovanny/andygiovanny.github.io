How to use Tomato Timers Ambience Studio?

Custom Ambient Sound Generator for Focus and Relaxation

Tomato Timer Ambient Sound Studio is a free background noise generator designed to help you focus, study, reduce anxiety, and sleep better. Mix and match 9 high-quality ambient soundscapes to create your ideal environment for deep work or relaxation.

Combine 9 High-Quality Ambient Soundscapes

Custom-tailor your atmosphere by mixing individual volume sliders. Our curated sound library includes:

  • Rain and Thunder: Relaxing heavy rain on a window, thunder rolls, and storm ambience.
  • Nature and Outdoors: Soothing ocean waves, mountain night sounds, seagull calls, and gentle wind.
  • Cozy Interiors: Warm campfire crackles, coffee shop background chatter, and mechanical keyboard typing noise.

How To Use It

  • Mix Sounds: Toggle any sound on and adjust individual volume sliders to blend your custom soundscape.
  • Set the Auto-Off Timer: Choose a custom countdown duration so the audio stops automatically when you take a break or go to sleep.
  • Use Presets: Click quick-start presets like Rainy Focus, Cozy Cafe, Campfire Night, or Ocean Breeze for instant sound combinations.
  • 100% Free and Browser-Based: No signup, downloads, or apps required. Runs continuously in your browser.
