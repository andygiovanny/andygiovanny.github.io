About your workbench

What is the Workbench?

The Workbench is your own page on TomatoTimers: a clean board where you arrange the timers and tools you actually use — a pomodoro next to a to-do list, a countdown pin, an ambience tile and your stats — each widget snapped neatly into place.

Where is my board saved? Do I need an account?

No account, ever. Your board layout and every widget's data live in this browser's local storage, on this device only. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Do the widgets stay in sync with the timer pages?

Yes. The widgets on the board are the same engines as the full pages, reading the same browser storage — a task you check off on the board is checked off on the timer page, and the streak you mark in the widget shows up in the streaks tracker.
