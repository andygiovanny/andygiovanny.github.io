How It Works

Focus Stats is the scoreboard for every TomatoTimers timer. Each timer records your finished sessions automatically — how long you focused, how long you broke, how many sessions you completed and which tasks you checked off — and all of it lands in one private log in this browser. No account, no upload, no setup: run any timer and this page fills itself in. The same four variables are measured for every technique (Focus, Breaks, Tasks Completed, Sessions), so the Pomodoro timer, 52/17, 20-20-20, Flowtime and the Custom Timer can be compared fairly on the per-technique breakdown. Switch between Today, This Week, This Month and All Time with the tabs above; the 14-day chart always shows the last two weeks regardless of the tab.

Where does the stats data come from?

Every TomatoTimers timer records your finished sessions automatically: focus time, break time, completed sessions and tasks you check off. All of it lands in one private log in this browser — nothing is uploaded and there is no account.

What do the four numbers mean?

Focus is your total focused work time in the selected period, Breaks is total break time, Tasks Completed counts to-dos you checked off, and Sessions counts finished focus sessions. The same four variables are measured for every timer technique, so Pomodoro, 52/17, 20-20-20, Flowtime and Custom Timer can be compared fairly.

Can I see stats per timer technique?

Yes. The per-technique breakdown shows how much of your focus time, sessions and tasks each timer contributed in the selected period, as colored share bars — so you can see at a glance whether you are mostly a Pomodoro person or a Flowtime person.

Why is my history empty? Can I import old data?

Stats start counting from the moment you use a timer that records them — timers did not keep a history before this page existed, so there is nothing to backfill. To preview what the charts look like with a busy history, use the Load example data button; it fills six weeks of realistic demo sessions that you can wipe anytime with Clear all stats.

Is my data saved? Can I move it to another device?

No account, ever. Your stats live in this browser's local storage and stay on this device only. Sessions and tasks recorded on your computer will not appear on your laptop — each device keeps its own log.
