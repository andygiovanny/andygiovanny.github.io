What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method developed by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s. It uses a timer to break work into focused intervals — traditionally 25 minutes — separated by short breaks. Our free online Pomodoro timer makes it easy to implement this technique with customizable intervals, cute themes, and soothing lofi ambience sounds.

How to Use Our Free Pomodoro Timer

  1. Pick a task you want to focus on
  2. Click Pomodoro to start a 25-minute focus session
  3. Work until the timer rings — no distractions!
  4. Take a 5-minute Short Break
  5. After 4 Pomodoros, enjoy a Long Break (15 minutes)
  6. Use the Loop button to automate the entire cycle
