The 20-20-20 rule

Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet (~6 meters) away for 20 seconds. It relaxes your eye's focusing muscle and reminds you to blink — the two things screens steal from you. The timer loops forever: the tomato wilts as your eyes tire, and when the window opens, look outside. That's the whole game.

Gentle by design

This page saves nothing and tracks nothing. No accounts, no stats, no streaks — just open it and rest your eyes. The warm dark palette keeps the screen soft in dim rooms, the same advice this timer exists to give.

How to use this timer

Press play and work. The tomato's energy bar drains over 20 minutes — when the window opens, look at the farthest thing you can see until the ring finishes. Snooze once if you're mid-thought; skip if you must, but your eyes keep the tired. Pair it with the to-do list below to give each 20-minute push a target.
